## 4. Account workflow

### Resolved access rules

- Public Donor and Hospital registration create `PENDING` accounts with matching profiles atomically, without automatic login. Admin accounts remain manually/out-of-band provisioned; no public Admin registration is provided.
- PENDING Donor/Hospital sessions are limited to status, permitted own-profile access, future password change, and logout. Donor own-profile read/edit is limited to full name, phone, address, area, and optional reported group; confirmed group stays staff-controlled. Hospital own-profile read/contact-only update is limited to contact name, phone, and address. Pending sessions have no operational actions; Admin Hospital reads require ACTIVE ADMIN separately.
- Login checks account role/status; protected implemented requests reload Account role/status. Suspended accounts cannot authenticate, and a protected request that encounters a suspended/missing/role-changed account destroys its session. Account suspension, audited reactivation, and staff actions are not implemented.
- Reactivation remains an audited staff action requiring a fresh login; previously revoked sessions must remain invalid. The persistent session store exposes account-wide revocation for future approved suspension/password operations.
- `/api/auth/me`, `/api/donors/me`, and the existing account-status surfaces stay within the 30-screen baseline; no screen was added.
- A shared concrete password policy is now defined for registration and future password changes in `requirements.md` and `api-design.md`. Password change at `POST /api/auth/password` remains an approved planned contract, not implemented. CSRF protection applies; public recovery is out of scope.
